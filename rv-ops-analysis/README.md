# Rebecca Vallance — AW24 Operations Analysis

**Scenario:** Operations Analyst supporting the buying and planning team at Rebecca Vallance for the AW24 season. The season launched mid-January 2024. At week 11 (end of March), management wants a read on sell-through, channel performance, and which SKUs need action before mid-season.

**Data covers:** 15 SKUs across RTW, Swim, Accessories, and Evening | 3 channels (Sydney, Melbourne, Online) | 116 transactions | January 15 – March 31, 2024 | Total season revenue: $110,940

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue by Category

| Category | Revenue | Share |
|---|---|---|
| RTW | $82,950 | 74.8% |
| Accessories | $9,500 | 8.6% |
| Evening | $9,250 | 8.3% |
| Swim | $9,240 | 8.3% |

RTW dominates at 74.8% of revenue. Evening punches above its weight — the Valentina Gown ($1,850 RRP) generated $9,250 from just 5 units, nearly matching Accessories despite a fraction of the transaction count.

### Store Performance

| Store | Revenue | Share | Gross Margin |

| Online | $43,930 | 39.6% | 69.5% |
| Sydney | $40,830 | 36.8% | 70.6% |

Online is the highest revenue channel. Sydney has the strongest gross margin, lifted by Evening (71.9%) and Accessories (75.9%) in the product mix. Melbourne's margin is softest due to a higher Swim weighting — Swim carries a 65% margin vs. RTW at 69%.

| RV-SWM-001 | Monaco One-Piece (Ivory) | 11 | 0.8 | 13.8 | Consider markdown |
| RV-SWM-002 | Monaco One-Piece (Black) | 16 | 1.1 | 14.6 | Consider markdown |

The Capri Knit Top has less than 1 week of stock remaining at current sell rate — immediate replenishment required. All three Swim SKUs are overstocked for an AW season; markdown is recommended to clear before mid-season.

---

### Operations Recommendations

1. **Replenish RV-RTW-008 immediately.** The Capri Knit Top has 0.6 weeks of cover. At 94.4% sell-through in week 11, a stockout before season end is a missed revenue opportunity.
2. **Monitor Arden Mini Dress closely.** Both colourways are below 3 weeks of cover. Confirm if replenishment is available from the supplier before committing.
3. **Initiate Swim markdown.** All three Swim SKUs are below 45% sell-through with 13–18 weeks of cover remaining. A 20–30% markdown in week 12 is recommended to clear stock before the season closes.
4. **Protect Sydney Evening allocation.** The Valentina Gown at 62.5% sell-through with only 8 units bought in is performing well for an Evening SKU. Consider whether additional units can be sourced for Sydney specifically.
5. **Investigate Greta Blazer Black underperformance.** At 37.5% sell-through vs. Camel at 50%, the colour is underperforming. Review in-store placement and consider a rack move or styling push before markdown.

```
├── data/
│   ├── rv_products.csv      ← 15 AW24 SKUs with RRP, cost, and initial stock
│   └── rv_sales.csv         ← 116 transactions across Sydney, Melbourne, Online
└── sql/
    ├── 01_schema.sql        ← Create tables (run first)
    ├── 02_analysis.sql      ← Core revenue and store analysis (5 queries)
    └── 03_advanced.sql      ← Sell-through, weeks of cover, margin, running totals (5 queries)
```

## Setup (DB Browser for SQLite)

1. Download DB Browser for SQLite — free at sqlitebrowser.org
2. New Database → save as `rv_aw24.db` in this folder
3. Execute SQL tab → paste and run `01_schema.sql`
4. File → Import → Table from CSV → select `rv_products.csv` (tick "First row is column names")
5. Repeat for `rv_sales.csv`
6. Run each query block in `02_analysis.sql` and `03_advanced.sql` one at a time
